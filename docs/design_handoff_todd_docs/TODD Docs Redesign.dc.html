TODD Docs, round 1
Docs in the Find look. The ten separate pages collapse into four tabs: Home, Opportunities, Documents and Knowledge. TODD reads incoming RFPs, scores them against your profile, drafts the proposal, checks it against the RFP's rules and sends it. While she drafts, she asks you for anything she doesn't know, and each answer is saved to Knowledge. Three directions for Home (1a–1c), then the flows, then iPad and iPhone.
Round 2: signed out, About, Help, single items1b is now Home. Signed out, it gets a guest banner and links to About and Help (2a–2c). Then the screen you land on when you open one item: a knowledge answer, a document, an image and a video (2d–2g), plus the knowledge answer and document on iPhone (2h–2i). TODD is the assistant everywhere in Docs: it reads, drafts, files and asks. Maya appears only when something is handed off to become a post.
