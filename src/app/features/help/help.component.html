Immediate help, on every page

Talk to TODD when you need a hand.

TODD is the AI assistant built into Docs. Move your mouse toward the bottom-right corner of the page and the Talk to TODD button will appear. Click it to ask about the document, task, or page you’re on. TODD can see which Docs page you’re using, so you can get help without leaving your work.

On a touch device, tap the bottom-right corner to show the button.
