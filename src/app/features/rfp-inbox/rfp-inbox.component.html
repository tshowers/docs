The inbox TODD watches for RFPs

Use a semi-dedicated inbox

Point your procurement alerts (OpenGov, Bonfire, King County, your city's portal) at an address like bids@yourcompany.com, and connect that. Every 10 minutes TODD reads only messages from the senders you list below, never marks anything read, and never moves or deletes mail. An inbox that's mostly alerts means TODD finds every RFP and nothing slips past in personal mail.
